The problem

A theme kills the credibility

Every piece is one-of-one — it exists once, sells once, never comes back. His Instagram worked because it read like a dealer's feed: fit pics, receipts, a wall of sold items. A stock storefront hides sold stock, and then he looks like a dropshipper.

The approach

An archive index, not a shop

Pieces are numbered stock entries. Sold ones stay up, stamped, as proof of volume.

Early-90s terminal meets fashion editorial: green on black, monospace, hard corners, no shadows. The product photos are the only colour.

Details

What got built

A custom layer over Shopify's Dawn — new Liquid sections, plus one stylesheet that re-voices the theme through its tokens.

Design notes

Two colours and one accent

Four values, named semantically — ink is always the text, paper always the field — so it inverts to a light colourway by swapping two numbers.

#0B0B0B
paper
#F0EEE6
ink
#8F8F8F
muted
#2BFF00
acid

The green is rationed: cursor, ticker, hovers, selection, active tab. It never sits under body copy — at that lightness it can't carry text.
