The problem

Work that isn't finished

Designing aircraft and writing the flight code that runs them, from scratch. No product to sell, no launch date — just a trail of builds, failures and fixes. Templates want a hero shot, testimonials and a buy button. None of that fits work in progress.

The approach

Build it like a dashboard

White field with a faint engineering grid, hairline borders, one blue accent, monospace for every label. Each build is a status card — active, planned, or coming — and the home page is a feed of where things stand.

Details

What got built

Six hand-written pages, one stylesheet. No framework, no build step.

System

Tokens, not one-offs

Design notes

Instrument, not interface

Three typefaces, three jobs: Space Grotesk display, IBM Plex Mono for every label, Inter for body. The mono does the work — once readings are monospaced, the page reads as a machine's output.
